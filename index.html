BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Transforming Retail Operations with
Predictive AI Intelligence

RetailInsight AI Limited is building a next-generation retail intelligence platform that helps UK retailers reduce food waste, protect margins, and make proactive operational decisions using privacy-first AI.

The Problem

UK retailers lose billions annually due to food waste, inaccurate demand forecasting, rigid pricing, and delayed store-level decisions.

The Solution

RetailInsight AI predicts waste risk up to 72 hours in advance and recommends pricing, replenishment, and donation actions before value is lost.

Privacy & Trust

Built using federated learning and explainable models, the platform protects sensitive retail data while aligning with UK regulatory expectations.

Commercial Impact

Retailers benefit from reduced waste, stronger margins, operational efficiency, and scalable multi-store deployment.
